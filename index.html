About the Project

This project plans to raise awareness about the ethical issues surrounding honey production and promote more sustainable and humane practices.

The Problem

Bees are exploited during the farming process of their honey, this also leads to environmental impacts and other negative consequences.

The Solution

This project aims to educate consumers about the ethical implications of honey consumption and encourage them to support bee-friendly and sustainable practices.

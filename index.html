About the Project

This project plans to raise awareness about the ethical issues surrounding honey production and promote more sustainable and humane practices.

The Problem

When Honey is collected through the use of bee farms, it leads to issues concerning the health of the bees, issues concerning chemicals in the honey collected, and issues concerning environmental impacts from introducing honey bees into a foreign habitat.

The Solution

This project aims to educate consumers about the ethical implications of honey consumption and encourage them to support bee-friendly, local, and sustainable practices.

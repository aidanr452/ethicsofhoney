Skill and reflection
Researching skills: This project has taught me to go in depth when researching websites. It is important to gather as much evidence as I can, and I developed this skill while working on this project.
Ethical thinking: Researching unethical honey practices has strengthened my ethical thinking because it has pushed me to identify, analyze, and respond to real issues. Reading ethically focused websites has helped me understand the ethical issues surrounding honey.
Focusing: This project has helped me focus on articles worth reading. They bring up good points, explain what is happening, and describe what we can do about it. The articles are interesting enough that I want to read them.

Project Summary and Future Plans

Looking Back and Ahead

Project summary: Throughout this project, we learned a lot about the production of honey and how it was harvested ethically and unethically. We also found many ways to support ethical honey practices. This project has been an enlightening experience for everyone involved, and helped us all learn and practice more skills.

Impact: Our Project helped reach a few people that hopefully inspired them to be more considerate in their honey choices.

What is in the future?: For the future, we hope that our research and our project as a whole will inspire more people to care about where their food comes from.
